GW2 account report for AI

Get markdown to give to an AI assistant: a summary of your Guild Wars 2 account, a plan for what you want to craft, or a list of what to sell to raise gold. Everything runs in this page: your key is sent only to api.guildwars2.com.

1. API key

Create one at account.arena.net/applications. It stays in this browser and is only sent to api.guildwars2.com.

        3. Your report

        Copy one section

          Crafting plan

          Pick one or more things to make and get the steps as markdown for an AI assistant, then paste its changes back. No API key is needed; with one that has the inventories permission, tick “Use my own materials” to count what you already own, or “Sell spare materials for the remaining gold” to also list what to sell for the rest. The plan, holdings included, is saved in this browser and in its .json download. Forgetting the key removes the holdings from the saved plan.

          Items to make

            Selling options

            Materials can be crafted or refined, or promoted in the Mystic Forge, first when that raises more; only one such step is considered. Promotion yields are random, so their quantities and gold are averages, and they use Spirit Shards. Looking up recipes takes about 20 seconds. What is read to sell is not saved with your plan.

            Recipes and routes

            Paste the assistant's reply, or just its JSON update. The format is at the end of the plan.

              Raise gold

              Find what to sell from your material storage, bank and bags, as a markdown checklist. Needs a saved key with the inventories permission; characters adds bags and crafting levels, unlocks adds learned recipes, wallet counts Spirit Shards and tradingpost checks the delivery box. Bound items, Ascended and Legendary items and equipped gear are never listed. What is read is not saved with your plan.
